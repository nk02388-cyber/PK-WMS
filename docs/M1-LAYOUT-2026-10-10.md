# Zone M1 layout adjustment

The marked warehouse plan removes M1-20 and M1-22 from the two lower-right positions and adds four positions in the aisle. Existing M1-01 through M1-19 and M1-21 retain their original coordinates and identities.

| New position | Placement |
|---|---|
| M1-23 | Left column, immediately below M1-16 |
| M1-24 | Middle column, immediately below M1-17 |
| M1-25 | Left of M1-19 |
| M1-26 | Left of M1-21 |

M1 now has 24 selectable positions, a net increase of two. Old-building capacity increases from 523 to 525; combined capacity is 1,014. The original fixed-capacity policy remains in place. Geometry changes and masks cover the retired CAD cells in the overview and desktop/mobile zoom views. New cells also appear as non-interactive context in other zone views.

This change does not migrate or delete stored pallet inventory or movement history. New positions keep distinct identifiers; retired identifiers are not reused. Production verification makes no inventory writes.

Validation: 102 Node regression tests passed. Browser fixtures at 1440, 768 and 390 pixels checked 24 M1 cells, absence of the retired identifiers, visible new cells, all 16 navigation menus, and stock editing/withdrawal/return form focus and overflow. Desktop/mobile screenshots were reviewed. Geometry tests verify non-overlapping cells, unchanged remaining M1 coordinates, correct placement, and unchanged unrelated zones.
